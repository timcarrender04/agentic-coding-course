Welcome — how this course works

This is a self-paced course delivered in 3 phases of 6 weeks each. You move at your own speed — progression is gated by completing labs, not by attendance.

The three pillars

  • Self-paced content. Every lesson is a hands-on learning object. Work in order; each completed lab unlocks the next lesson.
  • A lab for every lesson. Each lesson ends with a lab that produces a verifiable artifact — a screenshot, pasted command output, or a repo link.
  • Live blocks twice a week (support, not a gate). Monday is an instruction block; Wednesday is a lab clinic, 6:00–8:00 PM. Recordings are posted here. Being ahead or behind the suggested pace is fine.

Suggested pace

The week groupings in the table of contents are a recommended pace, not a lock. They tell you roughly where to be each week and set the focus of that week's Monday instruction and Wednesday clinic.

Checks on learning

Each module ends with a Check on Learning quiz and a hands-on final checkpoint. Both must be completed to advance into the next module.
