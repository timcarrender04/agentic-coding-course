"Operation Uplink" · Read this before class
Saboteurs cut the station's uplink overnight. Four subsystems, four fragments, one beacon — and Docker ships next week on this wire.
Wed Aug 12  ·  6:00–8:00 PM  ·  Zoom  ·  Setup required BEFORE class

The mission: saboteurs hit Relay Station 4 overnight and cut its uplink. Four subsystems — the Antenna Array, the Relay Rack, the Log Archive, the Crew Deck — each hold one fragment of the console's restart sequence. Recover all four, arm the console, and fire three test packets at the beacon: three echoes home and the uplink lives. Every station is solvable with commands from Monday's labs 01:07–10. Some clues sit in files; some are running, hiding in the machine itself. Read the station — it always tells you.

How the hunt works

CREWS

2 per crew

Each pair gets its own Zoom breakout room and races every other crew. Your crew clears a station only when both of you hold its fragment.

ROLES

Swap every station

Driver shares screen and types while narrating · Navigator reads the briefings aloud and calls the next command. Swap at every station door — you each drive twice.

RULE #1

Both must fire

Everything runs on your own machine. Coach with words only — never type on your partner's keyboard. The final banner must come from both terminals.

Scoring
  • Post clears to main Zoom chat: CREW 3 — Station 2 clear (2/2)
  • Winner: first crew with both victory banners in main chat
  • Tiebreaker: fewest hint tokens · winners pick the debrief topic
Hints
  • Your crew has 3 hint tokens for the night (Zoom "Ask for Help" summons the instructor)
  • Unlimited free hint, always the same: read the briefing again — or read what your last command printed
  • The station talks back: when something doesn't work, it prints why. Read the message before asking

Train before tonight — the six uplink skills

If you can do these six things, you can restore the uplink. Each one comes straight from a Monday lab. Rusty on any? Re-run that lab this afternoon — that's the whole read-ahead assignment.

#Can you…Practice commandsFrom lab
1Find your machine's own network addressesip a01:07
2List every port your machine is listening onss -tuln01:07
3Turn a name into an address — and fetch a URLdig +short example.com · curl01:07
4Find one process in a crowded process tableps aux | grep name01:08
5Chain a counting pipeline, stage by stagegrep | cut -d: -f1 | sort | uniq -c01:09
6Read your own line of the user manifestgrep $USER /etc/passwd · cut -d: -f701:09–10
Skill 5 is the boss fight. Practice saying the pipeline out loud, left to right: "grep keeps the matching lines · cut slices out one field · sort lines them up · uniq -c counts the repeats." If you can narrate that sentence, the Log Archive will not stop you — and the same pipeline is waiting on the phase-gate at 7:15.

Rules of the station

Fair play
  • Don't enter ~/uplink-hunt before the hunt starts
  • The .machinery bay is sealed — snooping in it voids your completion code
  • Coach your partner with words only — never type on their machine, never paste an answer
  • No web searches needed or allowed — everything is in the stations and on this page
  • Notes, this handout, and your lab files: all legal
Safety
  • Everything happens inside ~/uplink-hunt/ — the game never asks you to touch anything outside it
  • Zero rm -r and zero sudo in this game — nothing gets deleted, nothing needs root
  • Wrecked something? bash setup.sh --reset rebuilds the station fresh in seconds
  • Something that should be running has gone quiet? bash power.sh restarts the machinery

What "uplink restored" looks like

The console prints a victory banner with a completion code in your terminal. Screenshot it and post it in Zoom chat — that's tonight's participation artifact, and both crew members must post one. Then catch your breath: the phase-gate starts at 7:15, and everything you just typed is on it.

Still owe labs? Tonight IS the review. Everything you type in the hunt is labs 01:07–10 material — finish and submit those plus the phase-gate lesson 01:11 in D2L by Sunday. Labs, not attendance, gate your progress to Week 5's Docker install.
