Agentic Coding

Phase 1 — Foundations
Get comfortable in the developer environment and with containerized workflows before writing any application code.
Weeks 1–6  ·  Modules 00–02  ·  27 labs  ·  Mon + Wed, 6:00–8:00 PM

Phase 1 is the foundation of Agentic Coding at Temple College Workforce Development, delivered in collaboration with ChiefOS. Over six weeks you master VS Code, the Ubuntu terminal, and Docker — the tools every later module depends on. Content lives in D2L Brightspace; progress is gated by completing labs, not by attendance. Twice-weekly live blocks support you while you move at your own pace.

How Phase 1 works

1

Watch

Every lesson opens with a 2–3 minute concept video that sets up what you're about to do.

2

Do

Every lesson has a hands-on lab — real commands on your own machine, building real things.

3

Submit

Each lab produces an artifact in D2L. Completing it unlocks the next lesson.

4

Get unstuck live

Monday instruction + Wednesday lab clinic. Labs — not attendance — gate your progress.

What your computer needs

Everyone: a 64-bit laptop with 8 GB RAM (16 GB recommended), 30 GB free disk space, reliable internet, and administrator rights to install software. Windows 10/11 with WSL2 (Ubuntu) or macOS 12+. No GPU required — AI models (Phase 3) run on the college's shared server.
Not supported: Chromebooks and tablets (including iPads). Phase 1 lives in a real Linux/Unix shell — you need a laptop you can install software on. Windows students may alternatively work entirely over SSH into the college Linux server.
